---
title: "oxc/misrefactored-assign-op"
description: "Disallow `a += a + b`, where the target is repeated on the right"
---

Changing `total = total + price` to use `+=` and forgetting to remove `total` from the right side gives `total += total + price`. That adds `total` twice.

The rule reports a compound assignment when the right side uses the same operator and the target is its left operand. For `+`, `*`, `&`, `|` and `^`, where the order does not matter, the target as the right operand is reported too. The target can be a variable or a property such as `cart.total`.

`--fix-suggestions` removes the repeated target: `total += total + price` becomes `total += price`.

## Examples

Reported:

{/* bad {"count": 3} */}

```js
total += total + price;
scale *= 2 * scale;
cart.remaining -= cart.remaining - used;
```

Not reported:

{/* good */}

```js
total += price;
scale *= 2;
cart.remaining -= used;

// A different operator, or an order that matters
total += total * rate;
remaining -= limit - remaining;
```
