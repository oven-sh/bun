RESEARCH OF D3, "rules-expressions": six rules on expression shape. Nothing here is in the tree.
no-cond-assign, no-constant-binary-expression, no-constant-condition, no-dupe-else-if, no-extra-boolean-cast, no-unsafe-optional-chaining.

The oracle is ESLint at the pin of src/lint/UPSTREAM_PORTED (/workspace/ref/eslint) and, for a case with `ext`, the parser of
typescript-eslint (/workspace/ref/tseslint); sh ../tools/eslint-oracle-setup.sh restores both.

probe/               A NATIVE BINARY: the six rules as they are to be written, on the tree and the wrapper records of
                     Parser::parse_for_lint and on the scanner of src/lint/tokens.rs. It links the rlibs of the debug build of
                     /workspace/wt/cli (built at be1ebe5295), as c3-rule-support-unification/probe does.
  build.py           /workspace/tools/lk python3 probe/build.py /tmp/rx/probe        (a few seconds; needs build/debug of the worktree)
  src/main.rs        probe <file>...        every statement and expression of the walk with the wrapper records that name it,
                                            the records that name no node of the walk (UNMATCHED), the symbols of the parse pass
                     probe six <file>...    `path(line,col): rule: message` of the six rules
                     probe dupe <file>...   no-dupe-else-if alone
  src/dupe.rs        the support every rule uses (the index of the records by operand, plain, parens, start_of_node with its two
                     shortcuts, start_of_place), operand_key on tokens.rs, and no-dupe-else-if
  src/six.rs         ES_GLOBALS, is_global from ParsedForLint::symbols, isConstant and isLogicalIdentity folded over a chain,
                     the other five rules, and the walk with every call site
  src/shims.rs       the native symbols a parse reaches (copy of c3-rule-support-unification/probe/src/shims.rs)
  Not the final text: String, index syntax, println and comments of more than one line, which the workspace refuses; an array
  that is a pattern is not told from a literal (linter.rs knows it).
