- Parts of `src/js_parser`, `src/lint`, `src/typecheck`, and `test/cli/lint/conformance/runner` are ported or copied from Microsoft's [typescript-go](https://github.com/microsoft/typescript-go) and [TypeScript](https://github.com/microsoft/TypeScript) projects. Both projects are Apache 2.0 licensed. The `UPSTREAM_PORTED` file in each directory names the upstream commits.
- `test/cli/lint/conformance/corpus` holds test cases and error baselines copied from [TypeScript](https://github.com/microsoft/TypeScript) and [typescript-go](https://github.com/microsoft/typescript-go). `test/cli/lint/conformance/UPSTREAM` names the commits. The license and notice files of both projects are in `test/cli/lint/conformance`: `LICENSE`, `NOTICE.txt`, `LICENSE.txt`, and `ThirdPartyNoticeText.txt`.
- Parts of `src/lint` are ports of rules from [ESLint](https://github.com/eslint/eslint), which is MIT licensed. The test cases in `test/cli/lint/rules` include cases from ESLint's own tests. `src/lint/LICENSE.eslint` holds ESLint's license.
