## Known differences of a parse without lint

A parse without lint is the parser of main: what it accepts, what it rejects and what it prints are those of
bc7a813b10 (its parser is the one of f4d755a9cf: no file under src/js_parser or src/ast differs). The lists below are where that parser differs from tsc 6.0.2 on TypeScript that tsc takes. They are
defects of main. This work does not fix them: a fix changes what every run and every bundle reads, and it costs the
type skipper its speed. Until this round, four test files (`test/bundler/transpiler/typescript-grammar*.test.ts`,
366 cases) asserted the other behaviour for a parse without lint. They are gone, and each case is a case of
`src/js_parser/parse/grammar_rows_tests.rs`, which reads the same source with a lint parse.

"Valid" means what it meant in round 2: tsc reports no parse diagnostic and its checker reports no grammar error.
Where the checker of tsc reports another rule for the form itself, the heading names its code.
