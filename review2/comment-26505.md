Status for this issue. The review of #36316 asked for this route: pass the file descriptors of stdout and stderr to `net.Socket`, as `stdio[3]` and above already do. I measured that route on main's binary by sending stdout and stderr through two extra `"pipe"` slots. `child.stdout instanceof net.Socket` is `true` with it, and it passes all 13 `child_process` tests of #37171 that fail on main today. The numbers, the open points and the other routes are in https://github.com/oven-sh/bun/pull/37171#issuecomment-5951296651. The work waits for a maintainer's decision on the layer.
