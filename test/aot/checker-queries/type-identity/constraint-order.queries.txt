# The order of `Schema` constraints does not change an identity. The constraints themselves do.
same: constraint-order.ts:3 identity `number & Schema.Min<1> & Schema.Max<9>` <=> constraint-order.ts:4 identity `number & Schema.Max<9> & Schema.Min<1>`
different: constraint-order.ts:5 identity `number & Schema.Min<1>` <=> constraint-order.ts:6 identity `number & Schema.Max<1>`
