`bun test` takes whoever imports a module out of its loader when a mock of the module ends, and when a mock is laid over what an earlier test file loaded. A mock of such an importer in a later test file comes ahead of its load and stands for the whole module. The mock that ends may be one of a built-in module, come ahead of its load (built-in) or laid over what an import had loaded (built-in-laid-over).
