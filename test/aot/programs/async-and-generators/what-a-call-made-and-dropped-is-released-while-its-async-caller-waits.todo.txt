Both are dead at the await, but a suspended frame is copied whole and comes back whole, and the collections of this program run while the function is on the stack, where the scan of the stack finds the address. A collection that runs while the function waits frees the Blob: the map of the suspended frame does not list it. The cure: words that are dead at a suspension are cleared there or not saved.
