Throws "Cannot assign to console.log because built-in objects are read-only in a compiled executable" where plain Bun stores. The compiler has lost the console altogether here (it comes back out of a reviver of JSON.parse), so it sees no assignment to these members, takes them for constants and locks them at start-up. That is the design: a member is never written until an assignment is seen, and what stays constant is locked. It fails loudly, with the member's name. Counting every store through a receiver nobody follows as a store to every escaped built-in object was built and measured and not landed: it makes both programs right, but one such store anywhere opens the whole program: Hono's build 75.8 -> 217.7 G instructions and +7 MB, 659 of its 2,805 constant members lost, node:http +2.3% instructions a request.
