A bug: plain Bun throws in none of the eleven cases; under --aot nine throw a TypeError. The compiler lets the prototype of a class gain members only where the text of the program says X.prototype with X bound to the class. Here the class is handed to a helper (applyMixins of the TypeScript handbook, a decorator called by hand, extend(Target, methods)), is held in an array or a map, or its prototype is reached through an instance; and toString and valueOf are refused as names of Object.prototype.
